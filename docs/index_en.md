### ✤ Brief biography 

Crina Saad (Bondre Ardelean) is a Palestinian-Romanian born in Canada.  
Her work has appeared in various journals, including *Canadian Literature*, *The New Quarterly*, *Headlight Anthology*, and *Scene48*. Crina teaches literature in Montreal.


### ✤ The collection
*Of Earth* was first published in **2015** in the journal [*eudia*](https://www.eudia.org/poetry-crina-bondre-ardelean-of-earth/).  
I warmly thank Crina for allowing me to publish this Italian translation alongside the original text and for encouraging me to bring this project to completion.

### ✤ The bilingual edition

A first bilingual edition of the collection, with the original text and the Italian translation on the same page, can be downloaded at the following [link](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/massimocannas/Of-Earth-Crina-Saad/main/docs/Crina_Saad_Of_Earth_bilingual.pdf).  
An edition with facing-page translation will be available in January 2027.

Below is the first poem of the collection, *The Speed of Earth*, presented with the original text and the Italian translation.

### ✤ 


<div class="poem-grid">
<div>
<h4>The Speed of Earth</h4>

you think you're quick<br>
earth is quicker<br><br>

her feet reach to yours<br>
the moment before you step<br><br>

she breathes into you<br>
the moment before you inhale<br><br>

she unveils herself to you<br>
the moment before you look<br><br>

and she makes extraordinary sense<br>
the moment before you puzzle<br><br>

how she serves you!<br>
how you mistrust her!<br><br>

testing her, when she has brought<br>
all devotion, in great simplicity<br><br>



</div>

<div>

<h4>The Speed of Earth (Italian translation)</h4>

tu pensi d'essere veloce<br>
ma la terra lo è di più<br><br>

il suo piede precede i tuoi<br>
appena prima del tuo passo<br><br>

lei respira in te<br>
appena prima che tu inali<br><br>

lei si svela a te<br>
appena prima del tuo sguardo<br><br>

e tutto ha un senso straordinario<br>
un attimo prima che tu ti confonda<br><br>

ah! come ti serve!<br>
e come sei malfidato!<br><br>

a testarla, lei che ti ha portato<br>
ogni riguardo, in tutta semplicità<br><br>



</div>
